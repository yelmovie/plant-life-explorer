## 메인 — 초록정원으로 출발!

```text
Instrumental soundtrack for a cozy botanical adventure game for elementary school children. Warm, curious and inviting, like opening a garden gate into a sunlit greenhouse. 104 BPM, gentle acoustic game music. Felt piano, pizzicato strings, marimba, soft wooden percussion and occasional flute. A memorable, playful melody within the first few seconds. Light footsteps and a feeling of discovery, never frantic or babyish. Clear melody, spacious arrangement, soft dynamics suitable for a classroom.
```
